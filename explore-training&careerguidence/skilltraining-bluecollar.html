Core Technical Trades Training

Electrician – Students will be trained in electrical wiring, fittings, load distribution, fault detection, maintenance work and electrical safety practices as per industry standards.

Plumber – Training includes pipe installation, water supply systems, sanitary fittings, leakage detection and repair work using modern tools and techniques.

Welder – Learners will gain hands-on experience in arc welding, gas welding, metal fabrication, cutting work and welding safety procedures.

Shuttering / Carpenter – Practical training on formwork, shuttering systems, wooden structures, measurements, alignment and finishing work.

Mason – Training covers brickwork, block work, plastering, tiling, construction basics and on-site working methods.

After successful completion, trainees receive a skill certificate and placement support.

Mechanical & Industrial Skill Training

Pipe Fitter – Industrial and commercial piping systems, pipe cutting, threading, alignment, jointing and installation practices.

Steel Fixer – Reinforcement bar cutting, bending, fixing techniques and structural safety standards.

AC Technician – Installation, servicing, gas charging, troubleshooting and preventive maintenance.

Painter – Surface preparation, spray painting, brush painting, colour finishing and quality control.

Certified practical workshops with placement guidance.

Facility Management & Support Workforce Training

Cleaner – Professional cleaning methods, hygiene standards, and workplace safety.

House Keeper – Facility maintenance, room management, and professional conduct.

Store Keeper – Material handling, inventory systems, and record keeping.

Helper – Site support, tool handling, safety awareness, and teamwork.

Certification with job placements across India and abroad.
