HR, Management & Office Operations Training

This training program is designed for candidates who want to build a career in Human Resources, office administration, and management support roles. Learners are trained on real corporate workflows, office systems, documentation handling, reporting formats, and professional workplace behaviour. The focus is on building confidence, discipline, coordination skills, and professional conduct required in modern organisations.

HR training includes recruitment support, interview coordination, employee record management, attendance tracking, basic payroll understanding, and internal communication processes. Management modules focus on team coordination, task planning, follow-ups, email writing, meeting handling, and performance monitoring. After completion, students receive a recognised certificate along with placement assistance at local, national, and international levels.

Sales, Accounts, Tally & Calling Training

This program prepares learners for sales, accounts, and business support roles across industries. Training focuses on customer communication, sales processes, lead handling, follow-ups, billing basics, and accuracy-based office work that is required in real business environments.

Tally training covers accounting fundamentals, data entry, GST basics, invoice preparation, record maintenance, and daily accounting operations. Tally calling focuses on professional calling techniques, customer interaction, and data verification. Certified candidates receive job assistance in India and international markets.

BPO, Call Centre & Corporate Job Readiness Training

This training program is designed to make candidates job-ready for BPO, call centre, and corporate support roles. Learners are trained in communication skills, customer handling, CRM tools, dialer systems, voice and non-voice processes, and professional workplace behaviour.

Training includes mock calls, real-time practice sessions, interview preparation, and confidence-building exercises to meet industry expectations. After completion, candidates receive certification and placement support for opportunities ranging from local companies to national and international organisations across corporate and service sectors.
